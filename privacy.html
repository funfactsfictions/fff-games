Privacy notice

Real or Fiction? and Forever Facts Daily · last updated 25 November 2026

Short version: we don't ask for your name or email, we use no cookies and no advertising trackers. Your game progress stays in your own browser. We count visits anonymously to see which videos bring players.

Who runs these games

The games on this site are run by the creator of the YouTube channels Fun Fact's Fictions and Forever Facts. For any question about your data, write to funfactsfictions@gmail.com.

Legal basis

Keeping your game progress in your browser is necessary to provide the game you choose to play. Anonymous statistics and server logs rely on our legitimate interest in running and improving a small free website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Links to other sites

When you follow a link to YouTube, Instagram, Facebook or TikTok, that service's own privacy rules apply.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what data we hold about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Because we don't collect names, emails or accounts, we usually hold nothing that can identify you. You can also complain to your local data protection authority; in Hungary this is the NAIH (naih.hu).

Changes

If the games add accounts, leaderboards or an app, this notice will be updated before that happens.
